# Icon Email Check

Icon Email Check is a tray app for macOS, Windows and Linux. It counts the unread email in your Gmail and IMAP accounts and shows the count on its icon. It notifies you of new email, and opens your inbox with a click.

- [Download the latest release](https://github.com/rrgmc/icon-email-check/releases/latest)
- [Privacy policy](privacy.html)
- [Source code and documentation](https://github.com/rrgmc/icon-email-check)

The app reads only what it needs to count unread email, keeps passwords and sign-in tokens in your computer's keychain, and sends nothing to anyone but Google or your mail server. The [privacy policy](privacy.html) says exactly what it reads.
